Wreckin' Raccoon is a VR interaction sandbox for Meta Quest developed at nDreams Orbital. Players cause chaos through a world packed with complex, interconnected interactive objects — with an antagonist NPC that responds emergently to player behaviour.

## My Role

- **Interaction Design** — Designed emergent interaction subsystems and implemented dozens of individual complex interactive objects for the VR sandbox
- **AI** — Implemented a semi-emergent state-based AI architecture for the game's antagonist NPC
- **Analytics** — Created gameplay analytics dashboards in collaboration with the nDreams shared technology group, building reusable infrastructure for future nDreams titles
- **Post-Launch** — Provided support across early access and post-launch development
